✨ Miracul212 is a carefully crafted Minecraft modpack experience focused on performance, visuals, and immersive gameplay, while staying lightweight and smooth. Built for Forge 1.20.1, it contains 125 curated components — 103 mods, 9 shaders, and 13 resource packs.

The pack features the exclusive nastias_miracle_stones_mod, creature additions from Alex's Mobs & Naturalist, the mechanical automation of Create, and a complete performance suite including Embeddium, ModernFix, and FerriteCore — so the pack runs smoothly even on mid-range systems.

Visit the official website and connect with other players on Discord.

⭐Key Features
🚀 Performance and Optimization

Optimized for smooth gameplay and high FPS, even on mid-range systems. Embeddium rewrites the rendering engine for maximum FPS, Oculus enables efficient shader support, FerriteCore dramatically cuts memory usage, and ModernFix speeds startup and improves tick rates. Rounded out by Entity Culling, Noisium, Starlight, BadOptimizations, Dynamic FPS, ImmediatelyFast, Memory Leak Fix, RRLS, Fastload Reforged, Rubidium Extra, and GPU Tape.

🎨 Visuals and Immersion

Enhances graphics, animations, and overall atmosphere. Entity Texture Features and Entity Model Features add variety to mob appearances, Continuity enables connected textures, and Skin Layers 3D, Prism, Chat Animation, Sound Physics Remastered, Chat Heads, Photon, CIT Resewn, and Polytone round out the atmosphere. Nine curated shaders cover every hardware tier — from lightweight BVS to powerhouse BSL v10.1.8 and Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3.

🌍 Gameplay and Content

Adds new mechanics, decorations, creatures, and immersive features. Create adds a complete mechanical engineering system for factories and contraptions. Alex's Mobs and Naturalist add hundreds of creatures. Farmer's Delight, Supplementaries, Another Furniture, Handcrafted, Traveler's Backpack, Exposure & Exposure Polaroid, Immersive Vehicles, New Cars, Modern Japan City, Deco Storage, GWoodworks, Dinnerware, Spudacious Shops, FazRoleplay Clothes, ClothingCraft, Cluttered, Moving Elevators, and Furniture Tools Refurbished add depth to building and decorating, while WorldEdit speeds up large builds. Nastia's Miracle Stones and its Unified Stones extension remain the signature exclusive mods.

🧰 Quality of Life & 🌐 Multiplayer/Social

Improves usability, interface, and everyday gameplay with JEI, Jade, Xaero's Minimap & World Map, AppleSkin, Vein Miner, Mouse Tweaks, Shulker Box Tooltip, Carry On, WI Zoom, Freecam, Spark, Clumps, Sit, Skin Restorer, and more. On the social side, Simple Voice Chat enables proximity voice chat, e4mc allows direct LAN-over-internet play and Essential rounds out the social toolkit.

⬇️Installation Guide
💡
Use the Modrinth App — it downloads Forge and all mods automatically.
🟢 Method 1 — Modrinth App (Recommended)
1
Download Modrinth App
Visit modrinth.com/app and install it for your OS.
2
Import the Pack
Click "+" → "Import" → select Miracul212_1_1_2.mrpack
3
Wait for Download
All mods download automatically. Takes 2–5 minutes.
4
Set RAM & Play
Go to Instance Settings → Java → allocate 8 GB RAM minimum. Click Play!
🔧 Method 2 — Manual
1
Install Forge 1.20.1
Download from files.minecraftforge.net → run installer → "Install client".
2
Extract the .mrpack
Rename Miracul212_1_1_2.mrpack to .zip and extract it.
3
Copy Overrides
Copy overrides/ contents into %appdata%\.minecraft
4
Download Mods
Download each mod from its URL in modrinth.index.json into .minecraft/mods/
# .mrpack structure Miracul212_1_1_2.mrpack ├── modrinth.index.json # 101 mod download URLs └── overrides/ ├── mods/ # pre-bundled .jar files ├── shaderpacks/ # shader packs ├── resourcepacks/ # resource packs └── options.txt # pre-configured settings
⚠️
Requires Java 17+. For best performance use GraalVM JDK 17. Allocate at least 8 GB RAM — Create mod needs extra memory.
